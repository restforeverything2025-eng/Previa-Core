# PREVIA Product Contract v1

**Status:** APPROVED  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** PREVIA Core

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the canonical Product contract used across the PREVIA ecosystem.

Every PREVIA application and service must use the same Product structure and meaning.

The contract defines the business representation of a product.

It does not define how the product is stored or displayed.

---

## 2. Ownership

### Google Sheets

Google Sheets is the source of truth for product data.

CMS is responsible for reading and writing product data in Google Sheets.

### PREVIA Core

PREVIA Core owns:

- Product domain model;
- Product business rules;
- Product validation rules;
- Product contracts.

### Clients

Clients consume Product data.

Clients must not redefine Product business rules.

---

## 3. Canonical Product

The canonical Product contains:

| Field | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | Yes | Permanent PREVIA product identifier |
| `sku` | Yes | Unique product code |
| `category` | Yes | Product category |
| `brand` | No | Product brand |
| `name` | Yes | Product name |
| `description` | No | Product description |
| `price` | Yes | Current product price |
| `currency` | Yes | Price currency |
| `status` | Yes | Product availability/status |
| `featured` | No | Featured product flag |
| `dateAdded` | No | Date product was added |
| `sortOrder` | No | Manual display order |
| `notes` | No | Internal product notes |

---

## 4. Product Identity

### `id`

Permanent internal PREVIA identifier.

Rules:

- generated by PREVIA CMS;
- must be unique;
- must never be manually changed;
- must never be reused.

### `sku`

Public product identifier.

Rules:

- unique;
- generated by PREVIA CMS;
- must remain stable after publication;
- clients may use SKU to identify a product;
- SKU must never be duplicated.

---

## 5. Category

Every Product belongs to a category.

Examples:

- Watches
- Jewelry
- Skarb
- Sale

Category names and identifiers must follow the canonical category definition.

A client must not invent alternative category meanings.

---

## 6. Brand

Brand identifies the manufacturer or fashion house associated with the product.

Brand is optional because some products may have no known brand.

The absence of a brand must not prevent publication unless explicitly required by validation rules.

---

## 7. Price

`price` represents the current selling price of the Product.

Rules:

- must be numeric;
- must be finite;
- must not be negative;
- currency must be explicitly defined;
- clients must not recalculate or reinterpret the price.

---

## 8. Currency

Currency identifies the currency of the current Product price.

Examples:

```text
UAH
EUR
USD
```

Currency handling belongs to Core/CMS according to the operation being performed.

Clients display the supplied currency.

---

## 9. Status

`status` represents the business state of the Product.

The exact allowed status values are defined by PREVIA Core validation rules.

Clients must not create new status values independently.

---

## 10. Media

Product images are owned by the Product relationship.

Media itself is stored outside Core:

```text
Google Drive
    ↓
PREVIA CMS
    ↓
GitHub published media
    ↓
Client
```

Images are identified through Product SKU.

A Product may have multiple images.

Image ordering is part of the published Product representation.

---

## 11. Product Availability

Availability is a business concept.

Clients must not independently decide whether a Product is available for purchase.

Availability rules belong to PREVIA Core.

The CMS provides the underlying product data required by Core.

---

## 12. Client Representation

A client may transform the Product for presentation.

For example:

```text
Core Product
    ↓
Website Card
```

or:

```text
Core Product
    ↓
Telegram Product Message
```

Such transformations must not change the underlying Product meaning.

---

## 13. Storage Representation

Google Sheets may contain additional technical or administrative columns.

Such columns do not automatically become part of the canonical Product domain.

Therefore:

```text
Google Sheets Product
        ↓
     CMS mapping
        ↓
Canonical Product
        ↓
      Client
```

Storage-specific fields must remain storage-specific unless explicitly added to the Product contract.

---

## 14. Single Source of Truth

The ownership model is:

```text
Product Data
    ↓
Google Sheets

Product Business Rules
    ↓
PREVIA Core

Product Images
    ↓
Google Drive

Published Product
    ↓
GitHub / data.js

Presentation
    ↓
Client
```

---

## 15. Forbidden Behaviour

The following is prohibited:

- duplicate Product business rules in clients;
- manually changing generated IDs;
- manually changing generated SKUs;
- creating alternative Product models for individual clients;
- storing independent authoritative Product copies;
- changing Product status semantics in the frontend;
- calculating business availability independently in a client.

---

## 16. Product Lifecycle

The canonical lifecycle is:

```text
Incoming
    ↓
Google Sheets
    ↓
CMS validation
    ↓
PREVIA Core validation
    ↓
Publication
    ↓
data.js
    ↓
Client
```

For future commerce operations:

```text
Product
    ↓
Availability
    ↓
Order
```

---

## 17. Versioning

This document defines Product Contract v1.

Breaking changes require a new major contract version.

Backward-compatible additions may be introduced without changing existing field meanings.

---

## 18. Final Rule

There is only one PREVIA Product.

CMS stores it.

Core defines its meaning and business rules.

Clients present it.

No application may create a competing interpretation of the Product.
