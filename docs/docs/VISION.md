# PREVIA Vision

## Purpose

PREVIA is a long-term independent platform for collecting, managing and selling curated products.

Technology is replaceable.

Business rules are not.

---

## Principles

### Independence

PREVIA must never depend on a single platform, provider or service.

Infrastructure may change.

Core remains.

---

### Simplicity

Every solution should be as simple as possible.

Complexity must have a clear reason.

---

### Evolution

PREVIA grows through small independent milestones.

Each milestone leaves the project in a working state.

---

### Business First

Business rules belong to Core.

Applications implement those rules.

Infrastructure supports them.

---

### Customer

A Customer belongs to PREVIA.

Authentication providers only verify identity.

---

### Data

Data has a single source of truth.

Synchronization never becomes the source.

---

### Responsibility

Each module has one responsibility.

Each responsibility has one owner.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Create a platform that can evolve for many years without rewriting its foundation.
