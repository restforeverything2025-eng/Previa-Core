## Contract Rules

SKU is immutable.

Images are ordered.

Category must exist in Core Categories.

Currency must exist in Core Currency.

Status must exist in Core ProductStatus.

Product does not know anything about UI.

Product does not know anything about Google Sheets.

Product does not know anything about GitHub.
